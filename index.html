Common questions

Quick answers before your visit.

Are you open at night and on weekends?

Yes. Atlantic Hospital provides medical services 24 hours a day.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the booking form on the contact page, call 07037849704, or message us on WhatsApp at 09053386559.

Can I get a DNA test?

DNA testing is available. Contact the hospital to ask about the process and timing for DNA testing.

Do you have an ambulance?

Yes. Call or message us to request ambulance support.
