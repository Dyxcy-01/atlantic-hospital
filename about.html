About us

A hospital built around families, open every hour of the day.

Our promise

Atlantic Hospital exists to make good healthcare easy to reach. We combine round-the-clock services with warm, respectful care so patients feel safe from the moment they arrive.

From women’s health and children’s care to diagnostics, treatment and pharmacy, our approach is centred on clear information, respectful communication and helping patients navigate their care.

Patient safety

Clear procedures and careful, respectful treatment.

Compassion

We treat every patient as we would treat family.

Availability

Care when you need it, including nights and weekends.
